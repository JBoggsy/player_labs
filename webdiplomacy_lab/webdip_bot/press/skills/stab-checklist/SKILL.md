# Stab checklist

Break an agreement only if every answer is yes:
1. `assess_deal` "we_betray" beats "both_honour" by **two or more expected centres**.
2. The gain lasts: after the stab, the ally cannot retake those centres next year
   (check `predict` for that power with stance hostile).
3. You do not need this ally against a stronger threat in the next two years.
4. No other power will treat the stab as a reason to gang up on you.

If you stab, commit fully: set the victim's stance to hostile, raise `center_values` for
their centres, and stop promising them anything. Do not announce the stab in advance.
