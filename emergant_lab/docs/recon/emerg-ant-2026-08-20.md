## 2. What are the objective, termination, and score contracts?

The deployed match has 16 agents, two teams, a fixed arena, forage goal 5, five
lives, three HP, and a 3,600-tick limit. Touch the enemy food cache to carry it; return
it to the home capture zone for a delivery. The cache immediately replenishes. Death
or vanish also resets carried food home. First unique colony at the goal wins; a
simultaneous tied goal draws. A combat wipe can end the game, and the clock awards a
win only to a unique delivery leader.

Evidence:
[`coworld_manifest.json:585-665`](https://github.com/Metta-AI/coworld-emerg-ant/blob/1ea732ad68793ac25c6f888285eb90e6bccb188d/coworld_manifest.json#L585-L665),
[`sim.nim:2035-2148`](https://github.com/Metta-AI/coworld-emerg-ant/blob/1ea732ad68793ac25c6f888285eb90e6bccb188d/src/ctf/sim.nim#L2035-L2148),
[`sim.nim:2839-2898`](https://github.com/Metta-AI/coworld-emerg-ant/blob/1ea732ad68793ac25c6f888285eb90e6bccb188d/src/ctf/sim.nim#L2839-L2898), and

## 3. What information and actions does a player receive?

Emerg-ant uses retained/delta Sprite v1. Static map and home caches are globally
visible; carried food follows carrier fog. Self and exact own aim are always known;
other agents, including allies, are fogged and their aim is fuzzed. Public pheromones
remain visible through fog. Input is a level-held bitmask; Button C is `0x80` for
grenades. `Player Ready` must not be sent by league players.

## 4. How do pheromones work?

Moving ants deposit every 24 ticks. Marks last 720 ticks, are capped at 512, and are
labeled scout or food according to carrier state. Fresh simultaneous enemy marks
cancel; a fresh mark erases older enemy marks in an 18-pixel radius. They are public,
adversarial evidence—not private communication.

Evidence:
[`sim_types.nim:481-496`](https://github.com/Metta-AI/coworld-emerg-ant/blob/1ea732ad68793ac25c6f888285eb90e6bccb188d/src/ctf/sim_types.nim#L481-L496),

## 7. What should be reused from Paintbot?

### Reuse or adapt

- Sprite-v1 framing, retained delta sprites, backlog draining, exact own-aim parsing.
- Self/player identity parsing and team markers.
- Walkability-derived episode map, clearance, connected components, topology, cover,
  route fields, and navigation goal validation.
- Combat state/action structure after re-deriving GV52 constants.
- Carrier return, escort, interception, and home-defense concepts after replacing their
  state inputs and scoring assumptions.

Representative Paintbot seams:
`paintbot_lab/paintbot/stencil_nim/protocols.nim:58`,
`paintbot_lab/paintbot/stencil_nim/protocols.nim:146`,
`paintbot_lab/paintbot/stencil_nim/protocols.nim:262`,
`paintbot_lab/paintbot/stencil_nim/perception.nim:56`, and
`paintbot_lab/paintbot/stencil_nim/worldmap.nim:1`.

### Replace

- Heart/planted-heart label parsing.
- Permanent heart retirement and absence/death inference.
- Multiple one-shot target selection and associated squad directives.
- Paintbot combat/lives/timing constants and generated-map campaign assumptions.
- Any assumption that communication is private or that pheromones are trustworthy.

Representative incompatible seams:
`paintbot_lab/paintbot/stencil_nim/belief_update.nim:13` and the heart-specific sections
of `paintbot_lab/paintbot/stencil_nim/perception.nim` and `strategy.nim`.

## Recommended foundation

Build a **lean Stencil-derived carrier/defender baseline** first: retained perception,
navigation, combat, cache pickup/return, and conservative home defense. Parse and trace
pheromones but do not follow them as doctrine yet. That yields a runnable artifact and
lets the first hosted evaluation measure basic delivery/navigation/combat separately.

Before implementation, James should choose between that attributable baseline and a
larger first version with explicit scout/carrier/escort/defender roles. The game docs'
suggested role split is guidance, not an engine contract, and no evaluation yet shows it
is the right strategy.

## Open questions

- Which first-player scope does James want: lean carrier/defender or full colony roles?
- Should the initial player merely observe pheromones, follow friendly food trails, or
  actively erase enemy trails? This needs an attributable hosted experiment.
- Which current opponents expose the largest gap? Resolve the live roster and run a
  targeted baseline evaluation after the first upload.
- Does proximity chat improve coordination enough to justify giving opponents the same
  information? Do not add it without a concrete hypothesis.
