Predictions — the falsifiability contract

If TRUE — confirms
No same-squad/epoch directive conflict; at least 90% of available Stencil artifacts commit and follow orders; commits exceed timeouts; and no epoch gap greater than one among concurrently alive squadmates persists for 90 consecutive ticks (two 45-tick rebroadcast intervals).

Decision rule (committed before running)

Read as: Confirm only if every true-side threshold passes. Refute if any false-side condition occurs. Otherwise call the result inconclusive, including if artifact availability is too low to inspect at least 22 of 24 Stencil seats.

Confounds controlled

  • Permanent elimination freezes a dead process's epoch: exclude ticks where a member is not alive rather than treating death as protocol drift.
  • Transient propagation is expected in an asynchronous shout protocol: the bound is two configured 45-tick rebroadcast intervals, not instantaneous equality.
  • Artifact omission can hide a seat: require at least 22 of 24 Stencil artifacts and report omissions.
  • Three games test the stressed protocol mechanism, not win-rate superiority.

Verdict

refutedTwenty-three of 24 Stencil artifacts were available; all 23 committed and followed orders, with 154 commits, 17 timeouts, and 25 resyncs. However, four same-squad/epoch directive conflicts appeared across all three episodes, and one live epoch gap persisted for 122 ticks. Trace-level root cause: updateConsensus stored a locked vote but continued to count and publish a newly recomputed choice after late proposals. v51 corrects that exact path.
