What's being tested

Nothing in gameplay. We re-simulate historical replays at their exact source versions, sample player-view frames evenly across five GameVersion eras, serialize every labeled entity without truncation, and count native Qwen tokens.

How — the instrument

Exact-version replay re-simulation plus an offline tokenizer-length audit.
Model: Qwen/Qwen3-0.6B-Base
Revision: da87bfb608c14b7cf20ba1ce41287e8de496c0cd
Era buckets: GV01-16, GV17-24, GV25-30, GV31-35, GV36+
Sampling: require and select exactly 1,000 evenly spaced frames per represented GameVersion; include early, middle, and late POV frames across available maps, seats, and high-performing policies
Serialization: semantic label with numeric placeholders + extracted numeric values + normalized position/size/self offsets + z/layer
No truncation; no added action tokens in the count

Predictions — the falsifiability contract

If TRUE — confirms
Every era's 99th-percentile observation is at most 8,192 tokens and the largest observation in the complete corpus is at most 16,384 tokens, leaving at least half of Qwen's 32,768-token context for history, actions, and future fields.
If FALSE — refutes
At least one era has a 99th percentile above 8,192 tokens or any complete observation exceeds 16,384 tokens. We then need a more compact entity grammar, salience filtering, or a non-text spatial encoder before training.

Decision rule (committed before running)

Read as: Confirm only if all five era buckets are present, every per-era p99 is <= 8,192, and the global maximum is <= 16,384. Otherwise refute. An incomplete era corpus is not a result.

Confounds controlled

  • A current renderer would rewrite old observations; re-simulate each replay with its exact recorded GameVersion/source commit.
  • Abundant recent replays could dominate percentiles; cap samples equally by GameVersion and report every era separately.
  • Initialization and end-card frames differ sharply from live play; stratify early, middle, and late frames and retain frame phase in corpus provenance.
  • Map size, team count, seat, fog, and action phase change entity count; sample across these strata wherever each era supports them.
  • Tokenizer drift changes the answer; pin the exact Qwen model revision.
  • The walkability sprite's pixel payload is excluded and explicitly requires a separate representation experiment; this verdict applies only to semantic entities and geometry.

Verdict

refutedExact-version replay reconstruction produced 11,168 observations across GV16, GV24, GV30, GV35, and GV40; 1,000 per version were sampled. The full serialization had global median 8,876, p99 30,297, and max 43,672 tokens. Every era failed the 8,192-token p99 limit. A diagnostic not used for the decision removed only human-only `fog` runs from the same frames: global p99 fell to 3,429 and max to 4,424. The largest raw frame contained 804 fog objects and fell from 43,672 to 2,052 tokens without them. Therefore the faithful all-label grammar is too long, while a bot-semantic filter is strongly supported as the next representation change.

Shown for your go-ahead before running. The if-true and if-false predictions differ, so the result can actually decide it — that's the bar for running.
