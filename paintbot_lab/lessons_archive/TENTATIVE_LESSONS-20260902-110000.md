## 2026-09-01 — League-health loop: replay hash mismatch at tick 1 was a config-echo gap, not determinism
- **The replay header must carry every config key playback branches on.** Every hosted S2 replay failed its hash check at tick 1. Brute-forcing hash-structure variants of the playback state at tick 1 (zero players) found the recorded hash equals playback's with `needsReregister` left out of the mix, i.e. `numAgents != 0` live and `== 0` at playback: `num_agents` was echoed only under paintball (`echoPaintballKeys`), and the hosted league sets it for seating. `gameHash`, playback leave handling, `resetToLobby`'s tick reset and the per-seat results collapse all key on it. Fixed in coworld-ctf `d179633f` with a fixture that reproduces the hosted shape.
- **Method that worked:** when a hash mismatches at the first tick with no players, stop looking at inputs/joins and enumerate the hashed fields; perturb each single field, then each config-driven *structure* switch (guards like `if numAgents == 0`). The structural sweep found it in one run after the field sweeps found nothing. Fixtures passing while hosted fails means "a config key the fixtures never set".
