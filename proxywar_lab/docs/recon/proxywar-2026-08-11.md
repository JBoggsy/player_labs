## Mission

Founding recon for `proxywar_lab`: understand (1) the game dynamics of Proxy War — a fork of OpenFront.io,
a real-time territorial strategy game — and (2) the implementation: the agent/coworld layer, protocols,
observation/action schemas, artifacts, and the code gotchas a competitive policy author must know.

## The one-paragraph model

Proxy War wraps the OpenFront RTS engine in a **menu-selection decision game**: every N game turns
(100 turns = 10 simulated seconds in league play), each seated policy receives an `AgentObservation`
plus an offered `LegalAction[]` menu and must return exactly one offered `LegalAction.id` (plus,
optionally and independently, one offered `deal_*` id in a second **diplomacy slot**). Policies never
emit raw game intents; the server validates every selection through the same
`AgentDecisionValidator → AgentRunner → GameServer` path
(`docs/PROXYWAR_AGENT_ARCHITECTURE.md:9-19`). The live league is a rotating-map free-for-all whose seat
count (2/4/8/12/16) tracks the number of champions; win = first to 80% map control, and the ladder is
win-rate EWMA'd into an OpenSkill-style rating. Structured deals (referee-observed, breakable promises)
are enabled league-wide.

## 1. Game dynamics (OpenFront core)

Fork note: squashed history ("Initial public ProxyWar release" `9875f48`), so upstream diffs aren't
recoverable from git. Key divergences are flagged inline below.

### Time and phases

- **1 tick = 1 turn = 100 ms** (`src/core/configuration/DefaultConfig.ts:115-117`;
  `src/server/GameServer.ts:795-800`). League decisions happen every 100 turns.
- **Spawn phase**: 300 turns (30 s) multiplayer (`DefaultConfig.ts:609-617`); post-spawn immunity
  50 ticks (`DefaultConfig.ts:31,167-177`). **In league play spawn is not a decision** — the runner
  fairness-assigns spawn tiles before any request (`coworld-adapter/docs/player-protocol.md:110-114`;
  `tests/server/AgentSpawnAssignment.test.ts:94-142`: quality floor 0.5, greedy maximin spacing).
- Engine is deterministic: all RNG seeded from `simpleHash(gameID)` (`src/core/GameRunner.ts:56`), and
  the coworld `seed` is encoded into the gameID (`coworld-adapter/src/coworld-seed.ts:9-43`).

### Resources — NO population/workers economy

This fork predates OpenFront's worker/population system. There is **no worker unit, no population pool,
no target-troop-ratio slider** (grep of `src/core` finds only vestigial names,
e.g. `StatsSchemas.ts:82`). The economy is:

- **Troops**: start 25,000 (human seats). `maxTroops = 2*(tiles^0.6*1000 + 50,000) + 250,000*Σ(city levels)`
  (`DefaultConfig.ts:816-848`). Growth per tick `(10 + troops^0.73/4)*(1 − troops/max)` (`:850-882`)
  — logistic: sitting at cap wastes growth.
- **Gold**: flat **100/tick** passive (`:884-893`) + trade ships
  (`75,000/(1+e^(−0.03*(dist−300))) + 50*dist` paid to BOTH port owners, `:314-320`;
  `TradeShipExecution.ts:171-217`) + trains (ally 35k/other 25k/self 10k per stop, `:279-302`) +
  conquest (100% of a bot/nation's gold, 50% of a human's, `:512-521`). Gold is bigint, uncapped.
  Coworld seats start with **200,000 gold** (`coworld-adapter/src/no-docker-coworld-episode.ts:825-847`).

### Territory & combat

- Attack commits a troop amount (menu offers **10/25/40%** ladders; neutral expand 10/20/35%
  — `src/server/agents/LegalActionBuilder.ts:158-254,862-864`). Troops leave your pool immediately.
- Combat math single source: `attackLogic()` (`DefaultConfig.ts:627-748`). Terrain
  magnitude/speed: Plains 80/16.5, Highland 100/20, Mountain 120/25. **Defense post: ×5 defense, ×3
  defender speed within range 30** (`:210-220`) — and its gun code is commented out
  (`DefensePostExecution.ts:56-98`); it is purely an aura. Fallout tiles defend ×3-5. Wider shared
  borders conquer faster (per-tick conquest budget scales with adjacency, `:750-765`).
- **Elimination**: below 100 tiles ⇒ full absorption (`AttackExecution.ts:364-393`). Pockets/enclaves
  are auto-captured every ~20 ticks (`PlayerExecution.ts:100-319`). Structures change hands with the
  ground (`PlayerExecution.ts:44-67`).
- Retreat from a player costs 25% of committed troops; from neutral, free (`AttackExecution.ts:184-233`).
- **Attacking auto-embargoes you** (defender-side, temporary 5 min) and auto-rejects your pending
  alliance request, relation −60..−100 (`AttackExecution.ts:83-93,149-169`).

### Structures (cost = `min(cap, 2^n * base)` escalation; upgrade by building within 15 tiles)

City/Factory/Port 125k base (Factory+Port share the count); Defense Post 50k (cap 250k); Missile Silo
flat 1M; SAM `(n+1)*1.5M` cap 3M, range 70→150 by level; Atom Bomb 750k (radii 12/30); Hydrogen Bomb
5M (80/100); **MIRV 25M + 15M × MIRVs launched globally by anyone** (`DefaultConfig.ts:338-479,393-405`).
Factories seed the rail network and are the only train spawners (`FactoryExecution.ts:34-47`).
**Warships are force-retired server-side** (`src/server/GameServer.ts:129-139`) and additionally disabled
in the coworld config — naval combat is dead; boats (transports, max 3 concurrent, `troops/5` each) are
the only navy that matters.

### Diplomacy (engine level)

- **Alliances last 3000 ticks (5 min)**, extension needs both sides in a 30 s window
  (`DefaultConfig.ts:587-589,1011-1013`). Attacking an ally is hard-blocked (`AttackExecution.ts:71-81`).
  **Betrayal** marks you traitor for 300 ticks: attackers vs a traitor lose only half the troops
  (`traitorDefenseDebuff 0.5`, `DefaultConfig.ts:158-166`) — betrayal has a real, immediate combat cost.
- Donations need friendship, 10 s per-recipient cooldown, default 1/3 of stock
  (`DefaultConfig.ts:553-558`; `PlayerImpl.ts:732-856`).
- Emoji/quick-chat are cosmetic to the engine (but LEGAL ACTIONS on the menu, public in replays, and
  never deal violations). Relations decay 0.05/tick toward 0 (`PlayerImpl.ts:600-650`).
- **No deals in core** — structured deals are an agent-layer construct (§4); core got only deterministic
  donation receipts to let the referee verify support deals (`Game.ts:681-698`, commit `2231fa9`).

### Win conditions

`WinCheckExecution.ts` every 10 ticks: **80% of land tiles (FFA)** / 95% team; optional game timer and a
hard 170-min cap declare the current territory leader the winner (`DefaultConfig.ts:597-602`;
`WinCheckExecution.ts:22-89`). Coworld episodes set `maxTimerValue: 60` (= 36,000 turns) as a bounded
territorial tiebreak (`no-docker-coworld-episode.ts:840-844`), and the decision-step cap ends most games
before that.

## 2. The decision contract (what our policy is written against)

### Wire protocol (WebSocket)

Connect to `COWORLD_PLAYER_WS_URL` (`/player?slot=<slot>&token=<token>`). Server sends
`{type:"hello", slot, protocol:"proxywar-coworld-v1"}` (undocumented — don't crash on it), then per
decision:

```json
{"type":"decision_request","requestID":"req_<24hex>","slot":0,
 "request":{"protocolVersion":"proxywar-agent-v1","agent":{...},"match":{...},
            "observation":{...},"legalActions":[{"id","kind","label","risk":{"level","score"},"metadata"}],
            "decisionSupport":{"actionIDsByKind","safeFallbackActionID",...},
            "responseContract":{...}}}
```

Reply:

```json
{"type":"decision_response","requestID":"req_...","selectedLegalActionId":"<offered id>",
 "selectedDealActionId":"<offered deal_* id, optional>",
 "reason":"...","confidence":0.72,"fallbackUsed":false,"llmPlannerDegraded":false}
```

At episode end: `{type:"final", slot}` — finish any `COWORLD_PLAYER_ARTIFACT_UPLOAD_URL` upload
**before** closing; the close is the terminal-work ack. Sources:
`coworld-adapter/docs/player-protocol.md` (all of it), `no-docker-coworld-episode.ts:283-474`.
Max inbound frame 256 KiB; `reason` truncated to 500 chars, deal id to 200.

### Timing (the dominant design constraint)

- **`max_decision_ms: 15000` on every league variant** (hosted 0.1.35 manifest). Server timer starts at
  send; on expiry the promise rejects and `decideWithSafetyFallback`
  (`src/server/agents/AgentLeagueMatch.ts:1363-1418`) plays a **RuleAgentBrain** move for you with
  `fallbackUsed: true`. A late reply is silently discarded. The reference policies therefore answer
  every request from a **standing plan** and refresh LLM reasoning in the background
  (`coworld-adapter/src/keystone-player.ts:10-13`; starter `PLAN_EVERY=6`).
- Episode wall clock: `episode_timeout_seconds` 1200 (2p) → 2400 (4p) → 3000 (8p) → 3600 (12p) →
  4500/5400 (16p); manifest root `episode_timeout_minutes: 100`. Commissioner enforces
  `max(300, 2×episode_timeout_seconds)` (`commissioner/commissioners/common/server.py:95-103`).
- Connect within `player_connect_timeout_seconds` (120 s) or the **whole episode fails** for everyone
  (`no-docker-coworld-episode.ts:232-245`).
- **No reconnect**: a dropped socket means every later decide() throws → rule-bot plays your chair for
  the rest of the episode (`:297-300,384-388`).
- Post-final linger: on k8s (`KUBERNETES_SERVICE_HOST` set) linger before exiting
  (`PROXYWAR_PLAYER_POST_FINAL_LINGER_MS`) — self-exiting pods caused whole-episode "pod not found"
  failures (league rounds 1127/1128/1130). Unconditional linger breaks `coworld certify`
  (`coworld-adapter/src/starter-player.mjs:46-78`).

### AgentObservation (schema: `src/server/agents/AgentTypes.ts:1074-1130`)

Top level: `agentID, clientID, username, profile, gameID, gameMode?, alivePlayerCount?, phase,
turnNumber, tick, ownState, visiblePlayers[], combat, nonCombat, strategic, memory,
tacticalAffordances?, objective, recentDecisions[8], recentCommunications?, mapInfo?{name,width,height},
opponentModel?, economy?, deals?, endgame?, notes[]`.

- `ownState` (`:40-65`): `troops, maxTroops, troopRatio, gold(string bigint), tilesOwned, tileShare,
  borderTiles, incoming/outgoingAttacks, alliance request counts, unitCounts, isTraitor, ...`.
- `visiblePlayers` = **ALL other players, no fog** (`AgentObservationBuilder.ts:317-320`), each with
  strength (`relativeTroopRatio` = my/theirs), relations (`sharesBorder, isAllied, isFriendly, relation,
  underSiege, alliedWithVisibleIds` — coalition edges), and **precomputed permission checks**
  (`canAttack, canRequestAlliance, canDonate*, canEmbargo, canBreakAlliance, ...`).
- `combat`: bordered/attackable IDs, weakest/strongest attackable target, incoming/outgoing attack
  details (NOTE: for incoming attacks `targetID` holds the ATTACKER, `AgentObservationBuilder.ts:480-483`).
- `nonCombat`: `buildOptions` — **at most ONE pre-scored placement per unit type** with rich metadata
  (frontier/economic/defensive value, nuke targeting analysis); boat options, alliance options,
  support/embargo options, emoji (≤6) / quick-chat (≤10).
- `strategic`: server-side advice — `priority, urgency, scores{expansion, economy, defense, offense,
  diplomacy, threat, idleTroops}, recommendedActionKinds, targetPlayerIDs` (`AgentTypes.ts:811-819`).
- `memory`: `recentActions[8], repeatedActionKind/Count, avoidActionIDs[]` — the server literally tells
  you what you're repeating.
- `deals` (ON in league): `decisionStep, incomingProposals[≤6], outgoingProposals[≤8], activeDeals[≤8],
  proposalOptions[≤8], rivalReliability[≤8]` (`AgentTypes.ts:1060-1072`).
- `economy` is flag-gated **OFF** in every shipped manifest — do not depend on it.
- `endgame`: `winner, leaderID/Name/TileShare, ownTileShare, turnsToTimer` — read this for timer-race
  decisions.
- Coworld seats all get the **identical `opportunistic` profile** (fairness fix 2026-07-12,
  `no-docker-coworld-episode.ts:881-893`) — there is no per-seat personality.

### LegalAction menu (generator: `src/server/agents/LegalActionBuilder.ts:88-693`)

28 kinds (`AgentTypes.ts:1147-1179`). Emission order (matters under truncation): retreat, attack
(`attack:<playerID>:<pct>`, ladder 10/25/40), neutral expand (`expand:terra-nullius:<pct>`), boat,
build/nuke (`build:<unit>:<tile>`, only if affordable), upgrade, donate, alliance
reject/extend/break, embargo, embargo_all, `alliance:<playerID>`, deal_*, target, quick_chat, emoji,
delete_unit, and **`hold` always last and always present** (`:96-105`).

- **Menu cap 96** (`:116`); with deals on, assembly gets +32 headroom then a reserved-quota truncation
  guarantees ≥8 diplomacy slots survive (`:1418-1470`; `AgentTunables.ts:173-192`). `deal_accept`/
  `deal_reject` pairs are never split.
- `intent` is stripped on the wire — you see `id/kind/label/risk/metadata` only
  (`ExternalHttpAgentBrain.ts:274-280`).
- Off-menu `selectedLegalActionId` ⇒ validator substitutes **hold**, stamped `validationFallbackUsed`
  (`AgentDecisionValidator.ts:79-97`; `AgentLeagueMatch.ts:506-522`).
- **An offered id can still fail**: the runner re-filters per participant in order — same-turn
  diplomacy conflicts and build-tile spacing conflicts can invalidate an action that was on your menu
  (`AgentLeagueMatch.ts:1177-1239`). Earlier-ordered participants win.
- Absence of an id IS the signal (cooldowns, affordability, feasibility). Never construct ids.

### The diplomacy slot (deals)

`selectedDealActionId` is a **free second action** applied in the same decision — answering a pact does
not cost your move (`player-protocol.md:64-77`). Rules: exact offered `deal_propose|deal_accept|
deal_reject|deal_withdraw` id; anything else (especially a game action id) is rejected and **dropped, no
fallback**; one deal action per decision — a `deal_*` in the primary slot kills the deal slot
(`AgentLeagueMatch.ts:524-546`). Proposals cooldown 3 steps / TTL 4 steps / max 2 open per ordered pair
/ max 6 active deals per agent; support amounts 50,000 gold or 5,000 troops within six steps
(`AgentDealManager.ts:96-137`).

Templates and obligations (`player-protocol.md:99-108`):

| template | obligated | referee test |
|---|---|---|
| `non_aggression_pact` | both | no validator-accepted hostile action in window |
| `trade_security_pact` | both | NAP + no new voluntary embargo |
| `joint_attack` | **proposer only** | confirmed attack/nuke on named third party (land attack ≥20% troops) |
| `support_request` | **accepting recipient** | confirmed donations reach the stated amount |

Key asymmetries: **accepting a joint_attack obligates you to nothing**; deals never create an engine
alliance, never block a legal move, and **never alter league rating** — reliability is measured and
published (`rivalReliability`, same-match only; abstention scores `null` not perfect —
`docs/SOCIAL_MEASUREMENT_CONTRACT.md:143-152`), and shipped policies punish observed breaches
(commit `392a3c5`; `tests/coworld/StarterDealPosture.test.ts:507-598`). `reason` text is viewer-only —
**never shown to other players**; there is no persuasion channel
(`tests/server/AgentDealSlot.test.ts:571`). A documented live exploit pattern: template-mismatched
promises (e.g. NAP instead of trade-security, then embargo) are technically fulfillable while
behaving adversarially (`docs/SOCIAL_MEASUREMENT_REPORT_2026-08-08.md:124-138`).

## 4. Baselines, brains, and the strategy prior

Built-in brains (`src/scripts/ai-agent-league-smoke.ts` wiring): `rule` (deterministic profile cascade —
also the universal fallback), `mock-llm` (protocol fixture), `real-llm`/`codex-cli` (per-decision LLM
selector), `planner` (deterministic Commander stub + 23k-line `FrontierPolicyExecutor` — the house
baseline), `planner-codex-cli`/`planner-claude-cli` (LLM plan every 3 steps + deterministic executor),
`starter-bot` (in-process port of the public starter), and **Keystone**
(`coworld-adapter/src/keystone-player.ts` — the hosted house policy: background LLM Commander,
in-clock executor, profile aggressive, PLAN_EVERY=3, loud-failure rule).

**The public starter** (`proxywar-coworld-starter/llm-player.mjs`, 1384 lines) is
documentation-by-example of everything: Bedrock via `--use-bedrock` (platform service account, no keys;
hosted pods must use the `AWS_ENDPOINT_URL_BEDROCK_RUNTIME` sidecar or 403), plan JSON
(`focus/preferKinds/target/avoidTargets/dealPolicies/breakDealIDs`), obligation-fulfillment-first
move selection, anti-repeat guards, reliability gate 0.5, one-proposal-per-recipient/template with a
60-decision retry, and loud degradation (`fallbackUsed`/`llmPlannerDegraded` — the platform NEVER
infers these; a v1 Bedrock seat once failed silently for 60+ rounds, `no-docker-coworld-episode.ts:454-459`).

**Measured weaknesses of the house agent** (each an OFF-by-default tunable documenting a real failure,
`src/server/agents/AgentTunables.ts`): diplomacy starvation under the 96-cap; death-spiral passivity
when weakest; dominance inertia (expanding instead of converting a winning share); flat 25% attack
commitment that never cracks a defended core; naval blindness (no bordered rival ⇒ freeze; banked 10.3M
troops while the leader walked to 84.9%); gold hoarding (96M idle); no coalition play ("in every
analyzed loss the agent co-farmed the minors — feeding the leader both corpses — and ignored explicit
coordinate-attack proposals"). These are the exploitable holes in today's field.

**The strategy prior** (`docs/AI_AGENT_PLAYBOOK.md`): keep growing, don't sit at troop cap, city
first on safe interior, wide borders conquer faster, weak bordered targets first, defense posts only at
real vulnerable borders, ally to secure flanks/counter the leader, don't auto-extend every alliance.
The learning loop doc (`docs/PROXYWAR_AGENT_LEARNING_LOOP.md`) warns: blindly chasing leader tile-share
with neutral expansion measurably reduced final tile share.

## 6. Consolidated gotchas (ranked by expected pain)

1. **15 s decision deadline, no retry, late = discarded**; timeout/crash ⇒ rule-bot substitution with
   `fallbackUsed: true`. Architect standing-plan + async refresh; internal alarm at 5 s
   (`keystone-player.ts:732-736`).
2. **Score is 1/0** — optimize outright wins (80% control), not placement/territory.
3. **The diplomacy slot is free real estate** — a policy that ignores `selectedDealActionId` forfeits an
   action channel every decision.
4. **Never construct action ids**; feature-detect everything (deals from `responseContract` AND the
   menu; economy observation absent; warship kinds never appear).
5. **A disconnect is fatal** (no reconnect path); a failure to connect in 120 s kills the whole episode.
6. **Linger after `final` on k8s; don't linger in certify** (`KUBERNETES_SERVICE_HOST` gate).
7. **Be loud about degradation on the wire** — flags are never inferred (60-round silent-failure
   incident).
8. **An offered id can still be rejected** by post-offer conflict filtering — treat forced holds as
   normal noise, not bugs.
9. **Generalize across 8 maps and all spawn slots** — pool rotates by round, `episodeIndex` rotates
   seats. Don't tune to Pangaea.
10. **Deal attempts are consumed at selection** (no application callback in the policy process);
    proposal throttles are server-side AND starter-side (60-decision retry rule).
11. **Key social decisions by `playerID`/`dealID`, never name** — names are attacker-controlled,

## 7. Strategic implications for our first policy

What the recon says wins, given the field's measured weaknesses:

1. **Liveness first**: a policy that always answers in-clock, never disconnects, lingers correctly, and
   is loud about degradation clears a bar that has silently killed others for 60+ rounds.
2. **Deterministic executor + background planner** (the Keystone/starter architecture is correct;
   the differentiator is executor quality — the repo's own verdict is that a thin executor faithfully
   executing a good plan beats a deep heuristic cascade).
3. **Win-condition play**: the field exhibits dominance inertia and terminal passivity. Converting a
   winning share to 80% fast, and racing the timer via `endgame.turnsToTimer`, is where 1/0 scoring
   pays.
4. **Coalition play is an unexploited channel**: deals are on, joint_attack acceptance is free,
   the house agents co-farm minors and ignore coordination proposals. A policy that reads
   `rivalReliability`, keeps cheap promises, and organizes dogpiles on the leader attacks the field's
   documented blind spot.
5. **Anti-leader targeting**: 1/0 scoring means preventing anyone ELSE reaching 80% is as valuable as
   expanding; nukes/MIRV economics and the fallout denominator trick (fallout shrinks the win
   denominator) matter late.
6. **Scouting corpus**: stand up the league mirror immediately — per-decision data on every current
   opponent, free.
